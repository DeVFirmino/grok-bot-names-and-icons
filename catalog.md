# Catalog

35 icons, one bot for one part of life. The names and files are the library you already have. Attach the PNG as the avatar. Open the SVG if you want to recolour the tile.

Use the rows that match chats you already have. The tasks are lines you can paste. They say "I" and "my" so you can send them as they stand, with the text, numbers, or notes beside them.

Full grid: [library preview](assets/library/preview.png). Applying an icon: [step 3 in the README](README.md#3-apply-each-icon-in-that-bots-chat). Worked roster: [examples/before-after.md](examples/before-after.md).

The after screenshot also shows Papers and Postgrad. Those names belong to that roster. The library files are the 35 below. [Reading](#learning), [Study](#learning), and [Research](#learning) are the nearest rows when you want a name from this list.
