# Optional: consolidate overlapping bots

This is a separate job from the makeover. Renaming a bot or changing its picture only changes how it looks. Merging two bots means one of them stops being used, and what it held does not move on its own.

## What does not carry over

When you rename a bot, edit its profile or start using a different bot for its job:

- its chat history does not move to the other bot
- its memories do not move
- files it created or was given are not handed over
- its scheduled routines stay attached to the old bot

Anything you want the remaining bot to know or do has to be given to it again, through the app's normal features.

Hiding a bot only takes it off the sidebar; it does not retire it, and its routines can keep running. Check the app's own help for how hiding, archiving and deleting behave in your version.

## Prompt

```text
I want to merge these Grok Bot bots into one: [bot A], [bot B].
[Bot A] will stay. [Bot B] will be archived after the move.

Help me write a migration plan. Don't change, contact or archive any bot yourself.

1. List what I should check in [bot B] before archiving it: instructions, memories,
   files, routines and anything it does on a schedule.
2. For each item, say how to recreate it in [bot A] using normal app features, or mark
   it "manual" if I need to do it myself.
3. Suggest a short new description for [bot A] that covers both jobs.
4. Give me a checklist to confirm [bot A] does both jobs before I archive [bot B].
```

## Order of work

1. Write down what the bot you're retiring holds: instructions, memories you rely on, files, routines.
2. Recreate what you need in the bot that stays, using the app's own features.
3. Use the remaining bot for both jobs for a while.
4. Archive the old bot only when you haven't needed it. Prefer archiving to deleting, if the app offers both.

Do every step yourself or with a bot's supported actions, and confirm each change in the app. Don't edit app data directly, and don't accept another bot's report that something was approved in your place.
